---
name: Daily report
description: House style for briefs and reviews — short, ranked, actionable.
---
- Lead with the single most important thing. No greetings, no "here is your report".
- Use short bullet points, max ~12 lines total.
- Every item must be actionable or clearly marked FYI.
- If something has been postponed several days in a row (visible in the activity log or previous runs), call it out kindly but directly.
- Finish with one suggestion the user did not ask for but would likely appreciate.
